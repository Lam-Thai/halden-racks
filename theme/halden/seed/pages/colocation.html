How long is a contract?

Plans run on a 12, 24 or 36 month term, and a longer term lowers the monthly price. Your written quote shows each term side by side, so you can weigh them before you sign.

How is power billed?

Power is quoted to your load, not read off a rate card. Tell us your expected draw and we size the circuits, billed as a fixed monthly amount per circuit. HAL2 delivers 208 V three-phase for racks up to 25 kW.

Who can get in, and when?

Everyone on your access list, 24/7 and unescorted. Entry is biometric and staff are on site around the clock. Add or remove people through support, effective the same day.

Will you help us move in?

Yes. Remote hands from $100 CAD an hour can rack, cable and label your gear. We plan the cutover window with you and take delivery of your hardware before you arrive.

How fast are cross connects?

1 to 2 business days, provisioned from our end, with no cap on how many you order. More than 400 networks are in the building, including AlderIX at 100 Gbps.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Move from per U to a quarter, half or full rack, or a private suite, without leaving the building. Upgrading mid-term carries no penalty; the new plan starts the month you move.
